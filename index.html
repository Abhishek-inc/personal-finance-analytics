Smart money tips
1
Keep 3–6 months of expenses as an emergency fund before investing in equities.
2
If your savings ratio is below 20%, cut discretionary spending before adding new investments.
3
SIPs in index funds beat most actively managed funds over a 10+ year horizon.
4
Max out your 80C limit (₹1.5L) via ELSS funds — tax savings plus equity returns in one go.
5
Rebalance your portfolio once a year — equities grow faster and can skew your target allocation.
6
Never invest money you'll need within 3 years in equity — use FDs or liquid funds instead.
